# TALARIA grading scheme

Two catalogues, one spine. Every entry, real or invented, is first sorted by
the **loophole** it uses. Physics entries then carry a **standing** and a
**bill**. Fiction entries carry a **notability**, a **coherence** and a
**causality** mark. Every empirical statement on any page carries a
**confidence** and a **source tier**. The health checker rejects anything
outside these vocabularies.

## Loophole classes (shared)

| Class | Name | The move |
|---|---|---|
| **L1** | Faster in the same space | The object itself outruns light in ordinary spacetime |
| **L2** | Move the space | Spacetime is engineered to carry the ship; locally nothing exceeds c |
| **L3** | Take a shortcut | Two distant points are connected by a tunnel, fold, gate or jump |
| **L4** | Leave the manifold | The ship enters another space with different rules and re-emerges |
| **L5** | Send the message | Information, not matter, is claimed to beat light |
| **L6** | Not FTL | Filed here by habit: time dilation, hibernation, reactionless thrust, sublight epics |

A proposal that uses two moves (Mass Effect lowers mass to run L1 and uses
relays for L3) gets a dossier per move, or one dossier with the primary class
in the metadata and the secondary named in the text.

## Physics: standing

What kind of thing the proposal is, as the literature stands today.

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **K4** | Exact solution of the Einstein field equations, or an established result of quantum field theory, peer reviewed |
| **K3** | Peer-reviewed proposal with worked mathematics that is not an exact solution, or an exact solution whose central claim is contested in print |
| **K2** | Speculative physics with a named mechanism: a new field, particle or dimension, without consensus mathematics |
| **K1** | A claim with no worked mechanism, or a misreading of a real effect |
| **K0** | Refuted, retracted or shown to be an artefact |

K4 is a statement about the mathematics, not about feasibility. Alcubierre's
metric is K4 and has a bill nobody can pay.

## Physics: the bill

What the proposal needs that we do not have. A dossier may carry several.

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **B-neg** | Negative energy density; violation of one or more energy conditions |
| **B-mass** | Energy or mass beyond any conceivable engineering, even if positive |
| **B-new** | A field, particle or dimension not in the Standard Model or general relativity |
| **B-boot** | Needs FTL to make FTL: the structure must be laid along the route in advance |
| **B-caus** | Produces closed timelike curves, or requires a preferred frame to avoid them |
| **B-none** | No exotic requirement. Nothing faster than light sits here, and that is the finding |

## Fiction: notability

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **N1** | Canonical. Shaped how the public imagines FTL: Star Trek, Star Wars, Dune, Interstellar |
| **N2** | Significant. Widely known and mechanically articulated: the Expanse, the Culture, Mass Effect, Vinge |
| **N3** | Notable niche. Historically first, unusually rigorous, or an unusually clean example of a class |

The cap is roughly sixty works. A drive earns a dossier by having an
articulated mechanism, not by being famous.

## Fiction: coherence

How the work handles the physics it invokes. This is the grade that matters.

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **F4** | Engages. Names a real constraint and builds story around its cost. Vinge, Haldeman, Le Guin, Interstellar |
| **F3** | Acknowledges. States a limitation and applies it consistently: warp factors, navigators, relays only between relays |
| **F2** | Rule-bound. Internally consistent rules with no physical content: hyperspace lanes, jump cooldowns |
| **F1** | Handwave. Works when the plot needs it to |
| **F0** | Joke. The drive is the punchline and knows it |

F0 is not a low mark. The Infinite Improbability Drive is a perfect F0.

## Fiction: causality

Any FTL signal in a Lorentz-invariant world is a message to somebody's past.
What does the story do about it?

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **C-owns** | Admits the implication and uses it, or forbids FTL where it would bite |
| **C-dodges** | A stated rule prevents it: a preferred frame, jumps that are simultaneous for everyone, a hyperspace with its own clock |
| **C-silent** | Never comes up |

## Constraint verdicts

Every dossier scores every register entry. Physics vocabulary:

| Verdict | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **SATISFIES** | The proposal respects the constraint as stated |
| **VIOLATES** | The proposal requires the constraint to fail, and says so or can be shown to |
| **DODGES** | The proposal evades the constraint by an assumption the constraint does not cover (a preferred frame, a pre-built route, a different theory of gravity) |
| **SILENT** | The proposal does not address it and the literature has not settled whether it applies |
| **N/A** | The constraint does not bear on this class of proposal |

Fiction vocabulary, because a story cannot violate a theorem:

| Verdict | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **ENGAGES** | The work addresses this constraint on screen or on the page |
| **RULE** | The work has a stated rule that happens to answer this constraint |
| **SILENT** | Never addressed |
| **N/A** | Does not bear on this class of drive |

## Confidence

**[HIGH]** peer-reviewed and unchallenged, or primary text read directly.
**[MED]** peer-reviewed but contested, or a strong preprint, or a secondary
source of good standing. **[LOW]** preprint only, press account, or a fan
source with no primary confirmation.

## Source tiers

| Tier | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **S1** | The paper itself, or the primary text, film or game where the drive is defined |
| **S2** | Peer-reviewed review, monograph (Visser 1995, Lobo 2017), or an official technical manual |
| **S3** | Franchise wiki, author or showrunner interview, reputable popular science |
| **S4** | Press coverage |
| **S5** | Forum, fan speculation, unsourced blog |

Never blend the tiers. A Memory Alpha paragraph is S3 however confident it
sounds, and a physics claim resting on S3 is [LOW] until an S1 is found.
