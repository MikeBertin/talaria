# CAU-4: The preferred-frame escape

*Any FTL scheme that keeps full Lorentz invariance with no fixed FTL speed is a time machine.*

**Kind:** constraint · **Family:** CAU (Causality) · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-12

## The result

The preferred-frame escape. Faster-than-c signals are kinematically compatible with special relativity, which requires an invariant speed rather than a maximum one, and they avoid causal paradox when a soft breaking of Lorentz invariance (a property of the vacuum state, not of the fundamental laws) fixes the FTL speed in one frame. Paradoxes arise only from tachyons whose speed has no fixed value in any frame. The authors argue this for the Scharnhorst case and say statements of general validity are impossible without specifying the propagation

## What it forbids or costs

Any FTL scheme that keeps full Lorentz invariance with no fixed FTL speed is a time machine. Any that avoids the time machine has picked a frame, and must say what picks it

## Escape hatches

This is the escape hatch. Its cost is that special relativity is no longer the whole story for the FTL sector

## Who it bites

**Physics proposals**

- VIOLATES: [Meta relativity (Bilaniuk, Deshpande and Sudarshan 1962)](../theories/physics/meta-relativity-sudarshan-1962.md). The paper insists that the postulates of special relativity are strictly adhered to and gives class III particles no fixed velocity in any frame, which is exactly the configuration the register says is a time machine; no frame is picked and the paper does not consider picking one.
- VIOLATES: [Tachyons (Feinberg 1967)](../theories/physics/tachyons-feinberg-1967.md). Feinberg's theory keeps full Lorentz invariance with no fixed tachyon speed, which the register names as the one case that is a time machine; the proposal never picks a frame and its whole point is not to.
- DODGES: [Morris–Thorne traversable wormhole (1988)](../theories/physics/morris-thorne-1988.md). No preferred frame is chosen by the theory; the fixed mouths supply one by accident, and nothing fixes it once mouths move or a second wormhole exists.
- DODGES: [Visser's thin-shell wormholes (1989)](../theories/physics/visser-thin-shell-1989.md). No preferred frame is chosen by the construction; the fixed mouths supply one by accident.
- SATISFIES: [Lorentz-violating frameworks (the Standard-Model Extension and Hořava-Lifshitz gravity)](../theories/physics/lorentz-violating-frameworks.md). The entry demands that any scheme avoiding the time machine say what picks the frame; the SME says the vacuum expectation values of tensor fields (spontaneous breaking) and Hořava says the foliation, and Kostelecký and Lehnert give the conditions (small coefficients, a nonlocal completion above the breaking scale) under which the answer is consistent.
- SATISFIES: [The Scharnhorst effect](../theories/physics/scharnhorst-effect.md). This is the worked example CAU-4 was written from: the Casimir vacuum picks a frame, the FTL speed is fixed in it, and the authors argue the propagation is benign
- SILENT: [Alcubierre 1994: the warp drive metric](../theories/physics/alcubierre-1994.md). No preferred frame is proposed. The metric is written in the rest frame of the stars, but nothing in the construction says what picks that frame, and full general covariance is kept; CAU-4 says such a proposal must either pick a frame or accept the time machine, and the letter does neither.
- SILENT: [Bobrick and Martire 2021: physical warp drives](../theories/physics/bobrick-martire-2021.md). Mentions that causality "may be recovered at the expense of Lorentz invariance" and neither picks a frame nor says what would
- SILENT: [Brane-bulk shortcuts (Chung and Freese 2000, Ishihara 2001, Caldwell and Langlois 2001)](../theories/physics/brane-bulk-shortcuts.md). The papers never address what picks the frame; the register infers that the bulk supplies one, and the literature has not settled it for general brane configurations (Csáki, Erlich and Grojean and Greene, Levin and Parikh show brane motion and asymmetric warping generate genuine Lorentz violation on the brane, which is the same question from the other side).
- SILENT: [Entanglement signalling and the no-communication theorem](../theories/physics/entanglement-signalling.md). A working radio would need the preferred-frame escape (the frame in which collapse is simultaneous), and the proposals never say what picks it; relativistic quantum mechanics has no such frame and Peres and Terno show none is needed. The one worked case of the escape, the [Scharnhorst effect](../theories/physics/scharnhorst-effect.md), has plates to pick the frame; a radio has nothing
- SILENT: [Everett and Roman 1997: the superluminal subway](../theories/physics/everett-roman-superluminal-subway.md). The authors state that closed timelike curves could be avoided only by a preferred axis fixing the orientation of every tube, which is a CAU-4 frame; they propose no mechanism that would pick it.
- SILENT: [Fell and Heisenberg 2021: positive energy from hidden geometric structure](../theories/physics/fell-heisenberg-2021.md). No preferred frame is chosen or discussed
- SILENT: [Heim theory and the Dröscher-Häuser propulsion proposal (2004 to 2005)](../theories/physics/heim-theory.md). The papers claim Lorentz invariance under their dilatation and give the parallel space a light speed $nc$, but never say what frame the speed $nc$ is fixed in; the register's demand that a scheme avoiding the time machine say what picks the frame goes unanswered.
- SILENT: [Krasnikov 1998: the tube laid on the way out](../theories/physics/krasnikov-tube.md). Everett and Roman note that closed timelike curves could be avoided only by a preferred axis along which every tube is oriented, which is a CAU-4 frame choice; Krasnikov proposes no such thing and says nothing about what would pick it.
- SILENT: [Lentz 2021: hyper-fast positive-energy solitons](../theories/physics/lentz-solitons-2021.md). No preferred frame is chosen and the possibility is not discussed
- SILENT: [NASA Eagleworks warp field interferometer](../theories/physics/eagleworks-warp-field-interferometer.md). No frame discussed, though the boost-on-initial-velocity picture implicitly privileges the frame in which $v_i$ is measured
- SILENT: [Natário 2002: warp drive with zero expansion](../theories/physics/natario-2002.md). No preferred frame is proposed and general covariance is kept; the constraint asks what picks the frame that avoids the time machine, and the paper does not say.
- SILENT: [Retrocausal signalling proposals](../theories/physics/retrocausal-signalling-proposals.md). A working retrocausal signal could not be rescued by a preferred frame, since the ordering is reversed in the laboratory frame itself; the proposals do not address the point
- SILENT: [The EM drive](../theories/physics/em-drive.md). Not for FTL, but White's proposed mechanism, pushing against the quantum vacuum as a medium, would require the vacuum to have a rest frame. The paper does not say what picks it, and standard quantum field theory says nothing does
- SILENT: [The Hartman effect and the Nimtz tunnelling claims](../theories/physics/hartman-effect-and-nimtz.md). If the signal were real it would need the preferred-frame escape; the claim never says which frame the barrier picks, and the Casimir-type argument of Liberati, Sonego and Visser is not invoked
- SILENT: [The Mach effect thruster](../theories/physics/mach-effect-thruster.md). Momentum is said to be exchanged with the distant universe through the gravitational field, which means the universe's rest frame enters the dynamics; Woodward does not say whether this defines a preferred frame in the sense of CAU-4
- SILENT: [The OPERA superluminal neutrino (2011)](../theories/physics/opera-neutrinos-2011.md). The paper says any deviation beyond $10^{-19}$ "would point to Lorentz invariance violation in the neutrino sector" and stops there; it never says what would pick the frame, leaving that to the theoretical responses.
- SILENT: [Van Den Broeck 1999: the pocket geometry](../theories/physics/van-den-broeck-1999.md). No preferred frame is proposed or discussed.
- SILENT: [Warp drives in modified gravity](../theories/physics/warp-drives-in-modified-gravity.md). No preferred frame is proposed in either theory; torsion is localised in matter and conformal gravity is generally covariant.
- SILENT: [White et al. 2021: the worldline-numerics Casimir pattern](../theories/physics/white-casimir-nano-bubble-2021.md). No frame is discussed
