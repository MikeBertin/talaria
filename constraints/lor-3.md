# LOR-3: Front velocity is the signal velocity

*Every L5 claim built on a group velocity or a tunnelling time.*

**Kind:** constraint · **Family:** LOR (Kinematics) · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-12

## The result

Phase velocity and group velocity may exceed c; the front velocity of a signal cannot. Sommerfeld and Brillouin showed the wavefront in any causal medium travels at exactly c. Tunnelling "superluminality" (the Hartman effect, the Nimtz experiments, the single-photon measurement of Steinberg, Kwiat and Chiao at 1.7c) is not a genuine signal velocity: Winful shows the group delay in tunnelling is a lifetime of stored energy, not a transit time. The gain-assisted pulses of Wang, Kuzmich and Dogariu are classical interference between frequency components in anomalous dispersion. Neither is a signal beating its own front

## What it forbids or costs

Every L5 claim built on a group velocity or a tunnelling time

## Escape hatches

None; this is settled

## Who it bites

**Physics proposals**

- VIOLATES: [The Hartman effect and the Nimtz tunnelling claims](../theories/physics/hartman-effect-and-nimtz.md). The claim requires the front-velocity theorem to fail for evanescent modes. Winful shows the group delay is a cavity lifetime, that no point of the transmitted pulse leaves the light cone of the incident pulse and that the experiments contain no evidence of superluminality. LOR-3 holds the verified account
- DODGES: [Lorentz-violating frameworks (the Standard-Model Extension and Hořava-Lifshitz gravity)](../theories/physics/lorentz-violating-frameworks.md). Front velocity equals the species' maximal velocity $c_a$, and the Sommerfeld-Brillouin argument goes through with $c$ replaced by $c_a$; the settled result is respected in form and evaded in value.
- DODGES: [The Scharnhorst effect](../theories/physics/scharnhorst-effect.md). LOR-3's front-velocity argument assumes a causal medium with n → 1 at high frequency. The Casimir vacuum has n < 1 at all frequencies in the effective theory, and Barton and Scharnhorst argue the signal velocity is modified too. The register should note this as the one principled exception, of size $10^{-32}$
- SATISFIES: [Brane-bulk shortcuts (Chung and Freese 2000, Ishihara 2001, Caldwell and Langlois 2001)](../theories/physics/brane-bulk-shortcuts.md). The front velocity of a bulk signal is the bulk $c$; the apparent brane superluminality is a path-length effect, not a front outrunning its own light cone.
- SATISFIES: [Gain-assisted superluminal light propagation](../theories/physics/gain-assisted-superluminal-light.md). This is the entry's own worked example. The paper claims a group velocity above c and a front velocity of c, and says so; the FTL reading would violate LOR-3 and is refuted by the same analysis (K0)
- SATISFIES: [Kaluza-Klein and the hyperspace reading (Kaluza 1921, Klein 1926, ADD 1998, Randall-Sundrum 1999)](../theories/physics/kaluza-klein-hyperspace.md). Bulk and brane fields have hyperbolic equations with the bulk light cone as characteristic; front velocities are $c$.
- SATISFIES: [Tachyons (Feinberg 1967)](../theories/physics/tachyons-feinberg-1967.md). The tachyonic Klein-Gordon equation is hyperbolic with characteristics at c, so the front velocity of any disturbance is c even though the group velocity exceeds it; this agreement with LOR-3 is precisely why the field cannot carry a signal faster than light.
- SATISFIES: [The OPERA superluminal neutrino (2011)](../theories/physics/opera-neutrinos-2011.md). The bunched-beam runs of OPERA and ICARUS measure arrival of individual interactions against individual proton bunches, a front measurement not a group-velocity inference, and they agree with c.
- SILENT: [NASA Eagleworks warp field interferometer](../theories/physics/eagleworks-warp-field-interferometer.md). The planned time-of-flight experiment would time pulses through a test article; the entry's front-velocity result is what any such advance would have to answer, and it is never mentioned
- SILENT: [White et al. 2021: the worldline-numerics Casimir pattern](../theories/physics/white-casimir-nano-bubble-2021.md). The proposed test is a transit-time comparison for a pulse through a micron structure, exactly the measurement the entry warns about: group delay in a structure is not a signal velocity, and the paper does not distinguish them

**Fictional drives**

- SILENT: [Alien's Nostromo](../theories/fiction/alien-nostromo-hypersleep.md). The distress beacon, Ripley's log and the colony's two-week answer are signals with no stated speed; a signal limit applies and is never addressed
- SILENT: [Firefly's Verse](../theories/fiction/firefly-serenity.md). Waves are conversational across the system with no stated delay; a signal-speed limit applies and is never addressed
- SILENT: [Tau Zero's ramjet](../theories/fiction/tau-zero-ramjet.md). Contact with Earth is lost as the ship accelerates, but no fetched source shows the text treating the speed or cost of a signal home; the constraint applies and the work does not touch it
- ENGAGES: [Passengers' Avalon](../theories/fiction/passengers-hibernation.md). The message booth's nineteen years out and fifty-five for a reply is a light-speed signal limit stated on screen and accepted by the plot; the figures do not match the stated speed, and the film does not address the gap
- RULE: [Aurora's generation ship](../theories/fiction/aurora-generation-ship.md). The only link home is beamed light and light-speed messages, under the author's stated rule of no FTL communication; the late laser is a consequence of that rule
- RULE: [Avatar's Venture Star](../theories/fiction/avatar-venture-star.md). The companion's "Lightspeed Communications" are the stated rule for the ship's ordinary links; the film never shows a message between the stars
- RULE: [Revelation Space lighthuggers](../theories/fiction/revelation-space-lighthuggers.md). The author's stated rule of no FTL travel or communication keeps every signal at or below c; the decades of light-lag that structure *Redemption Ark* follow from it
- RULE: [Star Trek subspace radio](../theories/fiction/star-trek-subspace-radio.md). The signal has a stated finite speed through a stated other medium, with range limits and relays; the rule answers the front-velocity constraint by leaving ordinary space rather than by beating it
