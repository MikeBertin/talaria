# MAN-1: Extra dimensions offer no hyperspace

*"Hyperspace" as a separate space with laxer rules has no counterpart in physics.*

**Kind:** constraint · **Family:** MAN (Leaving the manifold) · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-12

## The result

Extra dimensions, where they appear in physics, are compact (Kaluza–Klein; large and compact in ADD, with Standard Model fields localised on a wall) or warped (Randall–Sundrum, a slice of anti-de Sitter space with the Standard Model on a brane). Shortcuts through the bulk are possible: Chung and Freese show a signal can leave the brane along a bulk null geodesic and return elsewhere sooner than any signal confined to the brane, as a non-inflationary answer to the horizon problem; Ishihara shows matter on the brane bends it so that such shortcuts exist, and calls the result apparent causality violation on the brane, with true causality defined by the bulk light cone

## What it forbids or costs

"Hyperspace" as a separate space with laxer rules has no counterpart in physics. Bulk shortcuts are a form of L3, remain inside the bulk light cone, and look to a brane observer like FTL with all of CAU-1's consequences unless the bulk supplies a preferred frame (CAU-4, an inference of this register, not a claim of these papers)

## Escape hatches

Brane bending is a real research direction; the price is that brane physics is no longer the whole story
