# CAU-3: Two devices make a time machine

*Wormholes and warp drives are not merely fast: they are time machines with an assembly step.*

**Kind:** constraint · **Family:** CAU (Causality) · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-12

## The result

A wormhole that can be created and maintained for interstellar travel can be converted into a time machine: take one mouth on a relativistic round trip. Everett shows a simple modification of Alcubierre's model, with no extra assumptions, in which two warp bubbles give closed causal loops

## What it forbids or costs

Wormholes and warp drives are not merely fast: they are time machines with an assembly step

## Escape hatches

Chronology protection (CAU-2) if it holds

## Who it bites

**Physics proposals**

- VIOLATES: [Alcubierre 1994: the warp drive metric](../theories/physics/alcubierre-1994.md). Everett (1996) shows that a simple modification of this exact model with two bubbles gives closed causal loops with no extra assumptions. Alcubierre himself anticipated that a spacetime with closed causal curves could be built on the same idea.
- VIOLATES: [Everett and Roman 1997: the superluminal subway](../theories/physics/everett-roman-superluminal-subway.md). Section 4 constructs the time machine from two non-overlapping tubes explicitly, with the ship leaving Earth at $t = 2D$ and returning at $t \approx 0$, and notes the analogy to Everett's two bubbles and the two-wormhole system.
- VIOLATES: [Fell and Heisenberg 2021: positive energy from hidden geometric structure](../theories/physics/fell-heisenberg-2021.md). Everett's two-bubble loop drops exactly the global hyperbolicity the paper leans on, and the Shoshany–Snodgrass gluing of two non-unit-lapse drives into a closed timelike geodesic reaches this class; the paper does not consider two devices
- VIOLATES: [Krasnikov 1998: the tube laid on the way out](../theories/physics/krasnikov-tube.md). The paper says the vehicles are square roots of time machines and describes the two-curve construction that closes a timelike loop; Everett and Roman carry it out with two non-overlapping tubes.
- VIOLATES: [Lentz 2021: hyper-fast positive-energy solitons](../theories/physics/lentz-solitons-2021.md). The soliton is a unit-lapse flat-slice metric of exactly the kind Everett modifies to close a causal loop with two bubbles; Celmaster and Rubin note that Shoshany has shown a Lentz drive, if built, "could be configured for time travel"
- VIOLATES: [Morris, Thorne and Yurtsever: the wormhole time machine (1988)](../theories/physics/morris-thorne-yurtsever-time-machine.md). This is the source paper for CAU-3: a maintainable wormhole plus one relativistic round trip of a mouth is a time machine.
- VIOLATES: [Morris–Thorne traversable wormhole (1988)](../theories/physics/morris-thorne-1988.md). This is the wormhole CAU-3 was written about: take one mouth on a relativistic round trip and the shortcut becomes a time machine (Morris, Thorne and Yurtsever 1988).
- VIOLATES: [Natário 2002: warp drive with zero expansion](../theories/physics/natario-2002.md). Everett's two-bubble loop uses only superluminal transport between flat regions plus a boost of the second bubble's exterior, which every member of the class provides; the profile of $X$ plays no role.
- VIOLATES: [Van Den Broeck 1999: the pocket geometry](../theories/physics/van-den-broeck-1999.md). The exterior is Alcubierre's, so Everett's two-bubble construction applies verbatim.
- VIOLATES: [Visser's thin-shell wormholes (1989)](../theories/physics/visser-thin-shell-1989.md). The Morris–Thorne–Yurtsever round trip applies unchanged; the paper names the causal constraints as an open question rather than answering them.
- VIOLATES: [Warp drives in modified gravity](../theories/physics/warp-drives-in-modified-gravity.md). Everett's two-bubble construction is a statement about the metric and a boost, and the metric is unchanged.
- SATISFIES: [Einstein–Rosen bridge (1935)](../theories/physics/einstein-rosen-bridge-1935.md). The conversion to a time machine needs a wormhole that can be maintained and traversed; this one pinches off before one crossing, so the assembly step never starts.
- SATISFIES: [ER = EPR (Maldacena and Susskind 2013)](../theories/physics/er-equals-epr.md). The conversion needs a wormhole that can be maintained and crossed; the bridge is non-traversable by assumption, and if it were traversable the conjecture would be wrong by its own statement.
- SATISFIES: [Fuchs et al. 2024: the constant-velocity physical warp shell](../theories/physics/fuchs-constant-velocity-2024.md). Everett's construction needs two superluminal bubbles; two of these shells are two slow spaceships
- SATISFIES: [Gao, Jafferis and Wall: traversable wormholes via a double-trace deformation (2017)](../theories/physics/gao-jafferis-wall-2017.md). The conversion by boosting one mouth is explicitly excluded because the deformation breaks the $H_L - H_R$ symmetry; this is a wormhole that cannot be made a time machine.
- SATISFIES: [Maldacena, Milekhin and Popov: traversable wormholes in four dimensions (2018), and the humanly traversable sequel (2021)](../theories/physics/maldacena-milekhin-popov-2018.md). The boost-one-mouth conversion fails because the through-time already exceeds the around-time by construction; the register's assembly step cannot close a loop.
- SATISFIES: [Wheeler's geons and quantum foam (1955–1962)](../theories/physics/wheeler-geons-and-foam.md). The conversion to a time machine needs a wormhole that can be maintained and crossed; these cannot be, so the assembly step is never reached.
- SILENT: [Bobrick and Martire 2021: physical warp drives](../theories/physics/bobrick-martire-2021.md). Cites Everett 1996 in passing and never examines two-device loops; its superluminal classes would fall to Everett's construction and its subluminal class would not
- SILENT: [NASA Eagleworks warp field interferometer](../theories/physics/eagleworks-warp-field-interferometer.md). Never mentioned
- SILENT: [White et al. 2021: the worldline-numerics Casimir pattern](../theories/physics/white-casimir-nano-bubble-2021.md). Two devices are never considered, though the proposed experiment is an array of many
