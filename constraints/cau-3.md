# CAU-3: Two devices make a time machine

*Wormholes and warp drives are not merely fast: they are time machines with an assembly step.*

**Kind:** constraint · **Family:** CAU (Causality) · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-12

## The result

A wormhole that can be created and maintained for interstellar travel can be converted into a time machine: take one mouth on a relativistic round trip. Everett shows a simple modification of Alcubierre's model, with no extra assumptions, in which two warp bubbles give closed causal loops
