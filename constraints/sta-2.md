# STA-2: The throat needs exotic matter

*Every L3 wormhole pays in negative energy, and the amount scales with throat radius.*

**Kind:** constraint · **Family:** STA (Stability) · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-12

## The result

A traversable wormhole throat must be held open by matter whose radial tension exceeds its mass-energy density, which no known material satisfies and which violates the energy conditions: Morris and Thorne's "exotic matter". Visser's thin-shell examples confine all of it to a delta-function layer at the throat, and the weak energy condition is violated there

## What it forbids or costs

Every L3 wormhole pays in negative energy, and the amount scales with throat radius

## Escape hatches

None classical; quantum fields at the throat, subject to ENE-3

## Who it bites

**Physics proposals**

- VIOLATES: [Gao, Jafferis and Wall: traversable wormholes via a double-trace deformation (2017)](../theories/physics/gao-jafferis-wall-2017.md). The throat is opened by matter with negative averaged null energy, as STA-2 says every traversable throat must be; here the exotic ingredient is a quantum field under a boundary coupling rather than classical exotic matter.
- VIOLATES: [Maldacena, Milekhin and Popov: traversable wormholes in four dimensions (2018), and the humanly traversable sequel (2021)](../theories/physics/maldacena-milekhin-popov-2018.md). The throat is held open by negative energy, as STA-2 says every traversable throat must be; here it is Casimir energy of Standard Model or dark-sector fermions rather than exotic matter, and the amount scales with the flux and the length.
- VIOLATES: [Morris, Thorne and Yurtsever: the wormhole time machine (1988)](../theories/physics/morris-thorne-yurtsever-time-machine.md). The throat is held open by exotic matter whose tension exceeds its density, as STA-2 states; the time machine inherits the throat unchanged.
- VIOLATES: [Morris–Thorne traversable wormhole (1988)](../theories/physics/morris-thorne-1988.md). This is the source paper for STA-2: the throat must be held open by matter with $\tau_0 > \rho_0 c^2$, which violates the energy conditions, and the tension scales as $1/r_0^2$.
- VIOLATES: [Visser's thin-shell wormholes (1989)](../theories/physics/visser-thin-shell-1989.md). This is the paper STA-2 cites for the delta-function layer: all exotic matter sits on the throat, the weak energy condition is violated there, and the total scales with throat size.
- SATISFIES: [Einstein–Rosen bridge (1935)](../theories/physics/einstein-rosen-bridge-1935.md). The throat has no exotic matter and, as STA-2 predicts for such a throat, it is not traversable: it pinches off.
- SATISFIES: [ER = EPR (Maldacena and Susskind 2013)](../theories/physics/er-equals-epr.md). The bridge contains no exotic matter and, as STA-2 predicts, is not traversable; the conjecture depends on this remaining true.
- SATISFIES: [Wheeler's geons and quantum foam (1955–1962)](../theories/physics/wheeler-geons-and-foam.md). These throats contain no exotic matter, and as STA-2 predicts they are not traversable: Schwarzschild pinches off, Reissner–Nordström pulsates behind horizons.
- SILENT: [The Mach effect thruster](../theories/physics/mach-effect-thruster.md). The book offers the wormhole term as the exotic matter STA-2 requires; no published calculation shows a throat held open by it, and the thruster papers do not address the throat
