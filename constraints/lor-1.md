# LOR-1: Nothing accelerates to c

*Every L1 scheme that proposes to push harder.*

**Kind:** constraint · **Family:** LOR (Kinematics) · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-12

## The result

A massive body cannot be accelerated to c: its kinetic energy grows without bound as v approaches c, so velocities greater than light have no possibility of existence for anything that starts below it

## What it forbids or costs

Every L1 scheme that proposes to push harder

## Escape hatches

Never having been below c (tachyons, LOR-2); changing the geometry instead of the velocity (L2, L3)

## Who it bites

**Physics proposals**

- DODGES: [Heim theory and the Dröscher-Häuser propulsion proposal (2004 to 2005)](../theories/physics/heim-theory.md). The craft accelerates in ordinary space at 1g and the papers explicitly rule out $c' > c$ in $\mathbb R^4$; the superluminal displacement is assigned to a space with $c(n) = nc$, which is the leave-the-manifold move and is outside what Einstein's argument covers.
- DODGES: [Lorentz-violating frameworks (the Standard-Model Extension and Hořava-Lifshitz gravity)](../theories/physics/lorentz-violating-frameworks.md). Each species has its own maximal attainable velocity $c_a$ and its energy diverges as $v \to c_a$, not as $v \to c$; a body with $c_a > c$ passes $c$ with finite energy, which Einstein's argument does not cover because it assumes one invariant speed.
- DODGES: [Meta relativity (Bilaniuk, Deshpande and Sudarshan 1962)](../theories/physics/meta-relativity-sudarshan-1962.md). Einstein's argument bounds a body that starts below c; the paper's class III particles are "created at super-luminary velocities" and never cross the barrier, which is the escape hatch the register itself names.
- DODGES: [Tachyons (Feinberg 1967)](../theories/physics/tachyons-feinberg-1967.md). The register lists never having been below c as the escape hatch from Einstein's 1905 argument, and Feinberg's pair-production picture takes exactly that hatch: nothing is accelerated through c.
- DODGES: [The OPERA superluminal neutrino (2011)](../theories/physics/opera-neutrinos-2011.md). The neutrino is massive and starts below c, so the claim could not be a body accelerated through c; the responses read it as a maximal attainable velocity above c (Coleman and Glashow kinematics), which is outside the Lorentz-invariant argument LOR-1 states.
- SATISFIES: [Alcubierre 1994: the warp drive metric](../theories/physics/alcubierre-1994.md). Nothing is accelerated to $c$; the ship is on a timelike geodesic with $d\tau = dt$ and zero proper acceleration, and the loophole is that the geometry moves, not the ship.
- SATISFIES: [Bobrick and Martire 2021: physical warp drives](../theories/physics/bobrick-martire-2021.md). States that "there is no known way of accelerating regular material beyond the speed of light" and that a superluminal shell can only be postulated, like a superluminal test particle
- SATISFIES: [Brane-bulk shortcuts (Chung and Freese 2000, Ishihara 2001, Caldwell and Langlois 2001)](../theories/physics/brane-bulk-shortcuts.md). Nothing is accelerated to $c$; the graviton is massless and the bulk geodesic is null.
- SATISFIES: [Einstein–Rosen bridge (1935)](../theories/physics/einstein-rosen-bridge-1935.md). Nothing is accelerated to or past $c$; all motion is timelike or null within the manifold.
- SATISFIES: [Entanglement signalling and the no-communication theorem](../theories/physics/entanglement-signalling.md). No massive body is pushed towards c; the particles travel to their stations at ordinary speeds
- SATISFIES: [ER = EPR (Maldacena and Susskind 2013)](../theories/physics/er-equals-epr.md). Nothing is accelerated to $c$; Alice and Bob move on timelike world lines and meet, if at all, inside the horizons.
- SATISFIES: [Everett and Roman 1997: the superluminal subway](../theories/physics/everett-roman-superluminal-subway.md). Inside the tube the ship propagates causally with $|dx'/dt'| < 1$ in the local Minkowski frame; the superluminality is a property of the opened cone as seen from outside.
- SATISFIES: [Fell and Heisenberg 2021: positive energy from hidden geometric structure](../theories/physics/fell-heisenberg-2021.md). Motion is geometric; the central observer is inertial and nothing is pushed through $c$
- SATISFIES: [Fuchs et al. 2024: the constant-velocity physical warp shell](../theories/physics/fuchs-constant-velocity-2024.md). $\beta = 0.02$; the Lorentz transformation used to build the comoving shift exists only below $c$, and subluminal speed is one of the paper's three stated ingredients
- SATISFIES: [Gao, Jafferis and Wall: traversable wormholes via a double-trace deformation (2017)](../theories/physics/gao-jafferis-wall-2017.md). Nothing is accelerated to $c$; the traversing signal is a light ray or a quantum that follows a null geodesic of the perturbed geometry.
- SATISFIES: [Gott's cosmic-string time machine (1991)](../theories/physics/gott-cosmic-string-time-machine.md). Each string moves at $v < c$ and no body is pushed to light speed; the observer's loop is timelike throughout.
- SATISFIES: [Kaluza-Klein and the hyperspace reading (Kaluza 1921, Klein 1926, ADD 1998, Randall-Sundrum 1999)](../theories/physics/kaluza-klein-hyperspace.md). Motion in the compact dimension is not a velocity but a mass: a particle with momentum $n/R$ around Klein's circle is a four-dimensional particle of mass $n/R$ obeying ordinary kinematics, and nothing is accelerated to $c$.
- SATISFIES: [Krasnikov 1998: the tube laid on the way out](../theories/physics/krasnikov-tube.md). The ship is always inside its local light cone; the return is superluminal only relative to the outside frame because the cone has been opened, not because the ship has been pushed.
- SATISFIES: [Lentz 2021: hyper-fast positive-energy solitons](../theories/physics/lentz-solitons-2021.md). Nothing is pushed through $c$; the Eulerian observers in the central region are in free fall with $d\tau = dt$ and the motion is geometric
- SATISFIES: [Maldacena, Milekhin and Popov: traversable wormholes in four dimensions (2018), and the humanly traversable sequel (2021)](../theories/physics/maldacena-milekhin-popov-2018.md). The traveller free-falls; the boost to $\gamma \sim 10^{12}$ at the centre is gravitational and the world line stays timelike.
- SATISFIES: [Morris, Thorne and Yurtsever: the wormhole time machine (1988)](../theories/physics/morris-thorne-yurtsever-time-machine.md). The mouth is moved at $v < c$ and the traveller moves at $v < c$ everywhere; the effect is purely a clock offset.
- SATISFIES: [Morris–Thorne traversable wormhole (1988)](../theories/physics/morris-thorne-1988.md). The traveller moves at $v \ll c$ throughout; nothing is pushed to light speed.
- SATISFIES: [NASA Eagleworks warp field interferometer](../theories/physics/eagleworks-warp-field-interferometer.md). The documents insist the craft "never locally breaks the speed of light"; the mechanism claimed is geometric
- SATISFIES: [Natário 2002: warp drive with zero expansion](../theories/physics/natario-2002.md). The Eulerian observers, including the ship, are in free fall inside their light cones; the exterior flows past. Nothing is pushed to $c$.
- SATISFIES: [Passage through the Kerr interior (Carter 1968)](../theories/physics/kerr-interior-passage.md). The traveller free-falls on a timelike geodesic at $v < c$ throughout.
- SATISFIES: [The EM drive](../theories/physics/em-drive.md). Even a working reactionless thruster would approach c asymptotically; its supposed advantage is unlimited delta-v, not a route past the limit
- SATISFIES: [The Mach effect thruster](../theories/physics/mach-effect-thruster.md). A working Mach effect thruster would still be a rocket without exhaust, approaching c from below with kinetic energy growing without bound; its advantage would be delta-v, not speed
- SATISFIES: [The relativistic rocket](../theories/physics/relativistic-rocket.md). This is the constraint the rocket lives inside. Its speed approaches c asymptotically and the kinetic energy grows without bound, which is why the mass ratio is exponential in the crew time and the bill is B-mass
- SATISFIES: [Van Den Broeck 1999: the pocket geometry](../theories/physics/van-den-broeck-1999.md). The pocket centre moves on a timelike geodesic with proper time $t$; nothing is accelerated to $c$.
- SATISFIES: [Visser's thin-shell wormholes (1989)](../theories/physics/visser-thin-shell-1989.md). The traveller crosses a flat face at any subluminal speed with no acceleration.
- SATISFIES: [Warp drives in modified gravity](../theories/physics/warp-drives-in-modified-gravity.md). The ship is on a timelike geodesic of the unchanged metric, and in Einstein-Cartan theory the torsion, localised in matter, does not affect it.
- SATISFIES: [Wheeler's geons and quantum foam (1955–1962)](../theories/physics/wheeler-geons-and-foam.md). Nothing massive is pushed to $c$; the geon's contents are light, moving at $c$, held by gravity.
- SATISFIES: [White et al. 2021: the worldline-numerics Casimir pattern](../theories/physics/white-casimir-nano-bubble-2021.md). Nothing is pushed toward $c$; the mechanism invoked is geometric
