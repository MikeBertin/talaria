# ENE-4: Van Den Broeck's pocket

*Does not remove the negative energy, only its quantity.*

**Kind:** constraint · **Family:** ENE (Energy) · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-12

## The result

A bubble whose interior is a 200 m pocket behind a femtometre-sized outer bubble reduces the Alcubierre negative energy from universe scale to about −2 × 10^30 kg, roughly a solar mass, with about 4.9 × 10^30 kg of positive energy alongside. The wall stays within about a hundred Planck lengths and the minimum curvature radius is about ten Planck lengths

## What it forbids or costs

Does not remove the negative energy, only its quantity. Structure a few orders of magnitude above the Planck scale is its own bill

None needed; this is itself an escape from ENE-3
