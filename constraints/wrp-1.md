# WRP-1: What a warp drive is

*The class definition.*

**Kind:** constraint · **Family:** WRP (The warp drive class) · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-12

## The result

What a warp drive is. Alcubierre: contract spacetime ahead of a ship and expand it behind, and motion faster than light as seen by outside observers is possible, but, as with wormholes, exotic matter is needed. Natário: the expansion and contraction are a marginal consequence of Alcubierre's choice; a warp drive with zero expansion exists, and the general class is any metric with unit lapse, flat spatial slices and a shift vector carrying the ship. Every warp proposal in this catalogue lives in that class and is priced by ENE-5

## What it forbids or costs

The class definition. A proposal outside it is not a warp drive and must say what it is instead

## Escape hatches

None needed; this entry defines the object

## Who it bites

**Physics proposals**

- DODGES: [Fuchs et al. 2024: the constant-velocity physical warp shell](../theories/physics/fuchs-constant-velocity-2024.md). Steps outside the class by design (non-unit lapse, curved slices) and gives its own three-part definition of a warp drive: geodesic transport, an empty passenger region and a comoving bounded bubble. Whether that is a warp drive or a spaceship with frame dragging is the WRP-2 question
- SATISFIES: [Alcubierre 1994: the warp drive metric](../theories/physics/alcubierre-1994.md). This is the entry's first citation: the class-defining example, with unit lapse, flat slices and shift $-v_s f(r_s)$ along the direction of travel.
- SATISFIES: [Bobrick and Martire 2021: physical warp drives](../theories/physics/bobrick-martire-2021.md). Places Alcubierre and Natário inside its scheme as Classes I and III and says what its own objects are, shells; Santiago, Schuster and Visser note that two of its metrics (non-unit lapse, conformally flat slices) lie outside the Natário class and must be judged separately
- SATISFIES: [Fell and Heisenberg 2021: positive energy from hidden geometric structure](../theories/physics/fell-heisenberg-2021.md). Unit lapse, flat slices, shift vector: the zero-vorticity subclass of the Natário class, as Santiago, Schuster and Visser label it
- SATISFIES: [Lentz 2021: hyper-fast positive-energy solitons](../theories/physics/lentz-solitons-2021.md). Unit lapse, flat slices, a shift vector: a Natário-class metric, as the conference reply itself says
- SATISFIES: [NASA Eagleworks warp field interferometer](../theories/physics/eagleworks-warp-field-interferometer.md). The Alcubierre metric verbatim, plus White's canonical rewriting of it
- SATISFIES: [Natário 2002: warp drive with zero expansion](../theories/physics/natario-2002.md). This paper is the class definition WRP-1 cites: unit lapse, flat slices, a shift vector field carrying the ship, and the demonstration that expansion is incidental.
- SATISFIES: [Van Den Broeck 1999: the pocket geometry](../theories/physics/van-den-broeck-1999.md). Unit lapse and Alcubierre shift, with a conformally flat rather than flat slice; the paper says it "can still be written in the 3 + 1 formalism" and the register's ENE-4 entry treats it as a warp drive. The departure from strictly flat slices is noted in the text above.
- SATISFIES: [Warp drives in modified gravity](../theories/physics/warp-drives-in-modified-gravity.md). The metrics studied are Alcubierre's and Van Den Broeck's; the class definition is unchanged by the choice of field equations.
- SATISFIES: [White et al. 2021: the worldline-numerics Casimir pattern](../theories/physics/white-casimir-nano-bubble-2021.md). The Alcubierre metric verbatim, with its shape function, York time and energy density
