# WRP-1: What a warp drive is

*The class definition.*

**Kind:** constraint · **Family:** WRP (The warp drive class) · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-12

## The result

What a warp drive is. Alcubierre: contract spacetime ahead of a ship and expand it behind, and motion faster than light as seen by outside observers is possible, but, as with wormholes, exotic matter is needed. Natário: the expansion and contraction are a marginal consequence of Alcubierre's choice; a warp drive with zero expansion exists, and the general class is any metric with unit lapse, flat spatial slices and a shift vector carrying the ship. Every warp proposal in this catalogue lives in that class and is priced by ENE-5

## What it forbids or costs

The class definition. A proposal outside it is not a warp drive and must say what it is instead
