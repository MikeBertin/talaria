# ENE-6: The Casimir floor

*The only negative energy anyone has ever made, and the only one anyone can point to, and it is tens of orders of magnitude short of any….*

**Kind:** constraint · **Family:** ENE (Energy) · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-12

## The result

The Casimir effect is real negative energy density between conducting plates, measured to about 5%. Its magnitude is tiny: energy density −π²ħc/720d⁴, about −4 × 10^-4 J per cubic metre at one micron, falling as the fourth power of the gap

## What it forbids or costs

The only negative energy anyone has ever made, and the only one anyone can point to, and it is tens of orders of magnitude short of any bill on this page

## Escape hatches

None; this is a floor, not a ceiling

## Who it bites

**Physics proposals**

- VIOLATES: [Morris, Thorne and Yurtsever: the wormhole time machine (1988)](../theories/physics/morris-thorne-yurtsever-time-machine.md). The paper points to Casimir-type effects as the only known negative energy; the register's measured Casimir density is tens of orders of magnitude below the throat's requirement.
- VIOLATES: [Morris–Thorne traversable wormhole (1988)](../theories/physics/morris-thorne-1988.md). The paper invokes Casimir-type quantum effects as the hint that exotic matter may exist; the register's measured Casimir density is some 35 orders of magnitude below the throat's $c^4/(8\pi G r_0^2)$ scale at 500 km.
- VIOLATES: [Visser's thin-shell wormholes (1989)](../theories/physics/visser-thin-shell-1989.md). Visser tried to source the edge stress-energy from a Casimir energy and could not; the measured Casimir density is tens of orders of magnitude below the requirement.
- VIOLATES: [White et al. 2021: the worldline-numerics Casimir pattern](../theories/physics/white-casimir-nano-bubble-2021.md). The proposal is precisely to use Casimir energy as the bubble's source; the entry records the floor as tens of orders short of any bill, and the pillar's factor of 3 to 5 over $-1.7 \times 10^{-6}$ J m$^{-3}$ does not change that
- SATISFIES: [Gao, Jafferis and Wall: traversable wormholes via a double-trace deformation (2017)](../theories/physics/gao-jafferis-wall-2017.md). The negative energy is Casimir-like in origin and magnitude, and the opening it buys is Planck-suppressed, consistent with the register's point that Casimir-scale negative energy buys nothing usable.
- SATISFIES: [Maldacena, Milekhin and Popov: traversable wormholes in four dimensions (2018), and the humanly traversable sequel (2021)](../theories/physics/maldacena-milekhin-popov-2018.md). The negative energy is Casimir energy of massless two-dimensional fields on a circle, $-q/8\ell$; the construction lives entirely within the Casimir-scale negative energy the register says is the only kind anyone has, and pays for it with a throat of length $q^2 l_p$ and a large flux $q$.
- SATISFIES: [The Scharnhorst effect](../theories/physics/scharnhorst-effect.md). The effect is driven by exactly the energy density ENE-6 records, about $-4\times10^{-4}$ J per cubic metre at one micron, and inherits its fourth-power falloff with gap
- SILENT: [Alcubierre 1994: the warp drive metric](../theories/physics/alcubierre-1994.md). The letter names the Casimir effect as the reason negative energy is not forbidden outright, and never prices it. ENE-6 puts the only negative energy ever produced at about $-4 \times 10^{-4}$ J per cubic metre at a micron gap, against wall densities that the inequalities push to Planck scale; the gap is tens of orders of magnitude and the proposal does not address it.
- SILENT: [Bobrick and Martire 2021: physical warp drives](../theories/physics/bobrick-martire-2021.md). Mentions zero-point fluctuations only to say Alcubierre walls would need Planck thickness; never names a source for the negative density its axisymmetric members need
- SILENT: [Everett and Roman 1997: the superluminal subway](../theories/physics/everett-roman-superluminal-subway.md). The negative energy is assumed to come from a quantum field in some state (footnote 21 says the conclusion is unchanged for massive scalar or electromagnetic fields); no source at Casimir scale is proposed and the gap is not addressed.
- SILENT: [Fell and Heisenberg 2021: positive energy from hidden geometric structure](../theories/physics/fell-heisenberg-2021.md). No source is named for the negative pressures the paper admits it needs
- SILENT: [Krasnikov 1998: the tube laid on the way out](../theories/physics/krasnikov-tube.md). No source is proposed for the negative energy; the Casimir floor is tens of orders of magnitude below any wall density and the paper does not address it.
- SILENT: [Lentz 2021: hyper-fast positive-energy solitons](../theories/physics/lentz-solitons-2021.md). The paper's purpose was not to need a negative-energy source; with negative regions restored it names none, and the Casimir floor is the only one there is
- SILENT: [NASA Eagleworks warp field interferometer](../theories/physics/eagleworks-warp-field-interferometer.md). The only negative energy on offer, the Casimir floor, is not invoked; the documents speak of "negative vacuum energy" from q-thruster physics without a formula that general relativity recognises
- SILENT: [Natário 2002: warp drive with zero expansion](../theories/physics/natario-2002.md). The paper does not invoke a source at all; the only negative energy ever made (Casimir) is tens of orders of magnitude short of any wall density, and the gap is not addressed.
- SILENT: [Van Den Broeck 1999: the pocket geometry](../theories/physics/van-den-broeck-1999.md). The Casimir effect is named as the reason the WEC may be violated quantum mechanically and never priced; the neck density of $10^{93}$ kg per cubic metre is about a hundred orders of magnitude above the Casimir floor and the paper does not address the gap beyond saying that generating enough negative energy remains a question.
