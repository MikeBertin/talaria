# WRP-2: A warp drive is a shell of matter

*Positive energy buys you a subluminal shell, which is a spaceship.*

**Kind:** constraint · **Family:** WRP (The warp drive class) · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-12

## The result

Any warp drive is a shell of regular or exotic material moving inertially. Bobrick and Martire give the general classification and present the first general model of subluminal positive-energy spherically symmetric warp drives, while superluminal ones require exotic matter. Santiago, Schuster and Visser find that the one case in the paper that satisfies the weak energy condition reduces globally to flat space and is not a warp drive at all

## What it forbids or costs

Positive energy buys you a subluminal shell, which is a spaceship. Superluminal warp is exotic matter by the authors' own classification

## Escape hatches

None; this agrees with ENE-2

## Who it bites

**Physics proposals**

- VIOLATES: [Fell and Heisenberg 2021: positive energy from hidden geometric structure](../theories/physics/fell-heisenberg-2021.md). Claims a superluminal configuration from positive energy; the entry says superluminal needs exotic matter, and the paper's own pressures confirm it
- VIOLATES: [Lentz 2021: hyper-fast positive-energy solitons](../theories/physics/lentz-solitons-2021.md). Claims a superluminal shell of positive-energy plasma, which the entry says needs exotic matter; the claim did not survive
- DODGES: [Warp drives in modified gravity](../theories/physics/warp-drives-in-modified-gravity.md). [Bobrick and Martire's](../theories/physics/bobrick-martire-2021.md) classification of superluminal shells as exotic is a general-relativistic statement; in Einstein-Cartan theory the shell is ordinary matter with spin. The classification is evaded by changing the theory, which the entry did not contemplate.
- SATISFIES: [Alcubierre 1994: the warp drive metric](../theories/physics/alcubierre-1994.md). In Bobrick and Martire's classification the Alcubierre bubble is a superluminal shell of exotic material moving inertially; the letter agrees on every point, including that the matter is exotic.
- SATISFIES: [Bobrick and Martire 2021: physical warp drives](../theories/physics/bobrick-martire-2021.md). The entry is built from this paper: positive energy buys a subluminal shell, superluminal needs exotic matter. The correction recorded in the entry, that the paper's explicit positive-energy example reduces to flat space, is a failure of the example not of the thesis
- SATISFIES: [Fuchs et al. 2024: the constant-velocity physical warp shell](../theories/physics/fuchs-constant-velocity-2024.md). It is the entry's shell: regular matter, positive ADM mass, moving inertially, subluminal
- SATISFIES: [Natário 2002: warp drive with zero expansion](../theories/physics/natario-2002.md). Bobrick and Martire's classification is built on the same shift-vector form; the zero-expansion drive is a superluminal shell of exotic material moving inertially and the paper says so.
- SATISFIES: [Van Den Broeck 1999: the pocket geometry](../theories/physics/van-den-broeck-1999.md). A superluminal shell of exotic material moving inertially, with the shell made microscopic; nothing in the classification is contested and the exotic matter is admitted.
- SATISFIES: [White et al. 2021: the worldline-numerics Casimir pattern](../theories/physics/white-casimir-nano-bubble-2021.md). Accepts that a warp bubble needs exotic matter and proposes Casimir energy as the exotic material
- SILENT: [NASA Eagleworks warp field interferometer](../theories/physics/eagleworks-warp-field-interferometer.md). Never engages the shell picture; a charged ring is positive-energy matter, which the entry says buys at most a subluminal shell

**Fictional drives**

- SILENT: [Campbell's Islands of Space (1931): where the word came from](../theories/fiction/word-hyperspace-campbell-1931.md). Whether the altered region is a shell of material moving inertially is never asked; the coil is inside the ship.
- SILENT: [Elite Dangerous: the frame shift drive (Frontier Developments, 2014)](../theories/fiction/elite-dangerous-frame-shift-drive.md). Bobrick and Martire's classification puts superluminal warp in exotic matter; the game's module burns hydrogen and says nothing.
- SILENT: [EVE Online: the warp drive (CCP Games, 2003)](../theories/fiction/eve-online-warp-drive.md). Bobrick and Martire's classification puts superluminal warp in exotic matter; the lore's depleted vacuum is exotic in the right direction and the quantity is never addressed.
- SILENT: [Futurama: the Planet Express ship's dark matter engines (1999 onward)](../theories/fiction/futurama-planet-express-drive.md). Bobrick and Martire's classification puts superluminal warp in exotic matter; the show's dark matter is heavy and the sign is never discussed.
- SILENT: [Star Trek: warp drive (1966 onward)](../theories/fiction/star-trek-warp-drive.md). Bobrick and Martire's result, that superluminal warp is exotic matter by classification, is never touched; the show's field is sourced by ordinary plasma.
- SILENT: [Stellaris: warp, hyperlane and wormhole (Paradox Development Studio, 2016)](../theories/fiction/stellaris-three-modes.md). Bobrick and Martire's classification puts superluminal warp in exotic matter; the game's warp drive is a component with a power cost and nothing more.
- SILENT: [The Orville: quantum drive (2017 onward)](../theories/fiction/the-orville-quantum-drive.md). Bobrick and Martire's classification puts superluminal warp in exotic matter, which the production's dysonium concedes; nothing on screen.
- RULE: [Death's End: curvature propulsion (Liu Cixin, 2010)](../theories/fiction/three-body-curvature-propulsion.md). Bobrick and Martire's classification gives positive-energy subluminal shells and nothing superluminal; the novel's drive is subluminal by rule and so sits on the allowed side of their line.
- RULE: [Star Citizen: quantum drive and jump points](../theories/fiction/star-citizen-jump-points.md). A subluminal shell moving inertially, which is the entry's positive-energy case; the lore insists on negative energy anyway, so the game is stricter with itself than the classification requires.
