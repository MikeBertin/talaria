# CON-3: The Krasnikov tube

*The honest L2 scheme, and it still pays the ENE-3 bill and the CAU-3 bill.*

**Kind:** constraint · **Family:** CON (Control) · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-12

## The result

The Krasnikov tube: a metric laid down along the outbound sublight journey that makes an arbitrarily long round trip take an arbitrarily short time by the traveller's clock and the home clock alike. Everett and Roman show the tube, like warp bubbles and traversable wormholes, needs unphysically thin layers of negative energy density and large total negative energies, and that a time machine can be built from two non-overlapping tubes
