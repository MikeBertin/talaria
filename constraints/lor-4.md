# LOR-4: The Scharnhorst effect and OPERA

*The one "measured" superluminal velocity in the literature was an instrument, and the one calculated is unmeasurable and causally harmless.*

**Kind:** constraint · **Family:** LOR (Kinematics) · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-12

## The result

The Scharnhorst effect: light propagating perpendicular to Casimir plates has a phase velocity exceeding c by a fraction of order 10^-32 for micron plates, falling as the fourth power of the gap, and too small by many orders of magnitude to measure. Liberati, Sonego and Visser show the effect is benign and constrained so as not to lead to causality violation. The OPERA superluminal-neutrino anomaly of 2011 was traced to a mis-seated optical fibre connection and an oscillator frequency offset; the corrected result, consistent with c, is the published paper. Nothing was retracted
