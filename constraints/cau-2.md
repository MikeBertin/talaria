# CAU-2: Chronology protection

*Any scheme that turns FTL into a time machine, which by CAU-1 and CAU-3 is all of them given two devices.*

**Kind:** constraint · **Family:** CAU (Causality) · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-12

## The result

Chronology protection conjecture: the laws of physics do not allow the appearance of closed timelike curves, because the expectation value of the stress-energy tensor grows very large as timelike curves become almost closed and the back reaction prevents them closing

## What it forbids or costs

Any scheme that turns FTL into a time machine, which by CAU-1 and CAU-3 is all of them given two devices

## Escape hatches

A conjecture, not a theorem; semiclassical, so a full quantum gravity might not obey it

## Who it bites

**Physics proposals**

- VIOLATES: [Morris, Thorne and Yurtsever: the wormhole time machine (1988)](../theories/physics/morris-thorne-yurtsever-time-machine.md). Chronology protection is the conjecture that the closed timelike curves this device produces cannot appear; Hawking's 1992 paper is the reply to this construction, and the two cannot both hold.
- VIOLATES: [Morris–Thorne traversable wormhole (1988)](../theories/physics/morris-thorne-1988.md). A maintainable wormhole of this class is convertible into a device that produces closed timelike curves; if chronology protection holds, it is the maintainable wormhole that fails, since the conversion step uses nothing beyond the wormhole itself.
- VIOLATES: [Visser's thin-shell wormholes (1989)](../theories/physics/visser-thin-shell-1989.md). The wormhole is maintainable and traversable, hence convertible into a device producing closed timelike curves; chronology protection and a maintainable thin-shell wormhole cannot both hold.
- DODGES: [Fell and Heisenberg 2021: positive energy from hidden geometric structure](../theories/physics/fell-heisenberg-2021.md). Section 3.3 argues that global hyperbolicity is a precondition of the construction, so the slices are Cauchy surfaces and closed timelike curves cannot form, and that horizons prevent manipulating the distribution past light speed; both are assumptions about a single drive, which is the entry's escape hatch rather than an answer
- DODGES: [Gott's cosmic-string time machine (1991)](../theories/physics/gott-cosmic-string-time-machine.md). Hawking's theorem assumes a compactly generated chronology horizon; Gott's horizon extends infinitely into the past because the strings are infinite, so the theorem does not apply, though Grant's divergence shows the conjecture still bites.
- DODGES: [Passage through the Kerr interior (Carter 1968)](../theories/physics/kerr-interior-passage.md). Carter's closed timelike curves are eternal features of the analytic extension rather than curves that appear, so chronology protection, which concerns their formation, is not engaged; the extension containing them is in any case not the interior of a real collapse.
- SATISFIES: [Brane-bulk shortcuts (Chung and Freese 2000, Ishihara 2001, Caldwell and Langlois 2001)](../theories/physics/brane-bulk-shortcuts.md). Every shortcut is a null geodesic of the bulk, so the full five-dimensional spacetime has an ordinary causal structure with no closed causal curves for the conjecture to act on.
- SATISFIES: [Einstein–Rosen bridge (1935)](../theories/physics/einstein-rosen-bridge-1935.md). The maximal extension contains no closed timelike curves; chronology protection has nothing to protect against.
- SATISFIES: [ER = EPR (Maldacena and Susskind 2013)](../theories/physics/er-equals-epr.md). The eternal black hole contains no closed timelike curves and the conjecture creates none; chronology protection has nothing to act on.
- SATISFIES: [Fuchs et al. 2024: the constant-velocity physical warp shell](../theories/physics/fuchs-constant-velocity-2024.md). No closed timelike curves can be built from a subluminal shell; there is nothing for chronology protection to forbid
- SATISFIES: [Gao, Jafferis and Wall: traversable wormholes via a double-trace deformation (2017)](../theories/physics/gao-jafferis-wall-2017.md). No closed timelike curves form; the coupling fixes the relative time coordinate of the two boundaries and the authors show the boost trick is unavailable.
- SATISFIES: [Kaluza-Klein and the hyperspace reading (Kaluza 1921, Klein 1926, ADD 1998, Randall-Sundrum 1999)](../theories/physics/kaluza-klein-hyperspace.md). The compactified and warped solutions have ordinary global causal structure with no closed timelike curves.
- SATISFIES: [Lorentz-violating frameworks (the Standard-Model Extension and Hořava-Lifshitz gravity)](../theories/physics/lorentz-violating-frameworks.md). Hořava's preferred foliation is a global time function, which excludes closed timelike curves outright; in the flat-space SME the causal structure is fixed by the concordant-frame light cones of the fastest species and closes on nothing.
- SATISFIES: [Maldacena, Milekhin and Popov: traversable wormholes in four dimensions (2018), and the humanly traversable sequel (2021)](../theories/physics/maldacena-milekhin-popov-2018.md). No closed timelike curves form; the authors state the long wormhole cannot be converted into a time machine.
- SATISFIES: [Wheeler's geons and quantum foam (1955–1962)](../theories/physics/wheeler-geons-and-foam.md). None of the geon, charged-throat or foam constructions produces closed timelike curves; nothing appears for chronology protection to forbid.
- SILENT: [Alcubierre 1994: the warp drive metric](../theories/physics/alcubierre-1994.md). The letter does not mention chronology protection. Hawking's conjecture would forbid the two-bubble time machine of CAU-3 by semiclassical back reaction, and whether it does is unsettled; no analysis of the Alcubierre chronology horizon exists.
- SILENT: [Bobrick and Martire 2021: physical warp drives](../theories/physics/bobrick-martire-2021.md). Notes in the introduction that superluminal motion "allows for closed timelike loops" and never discusses chronology protection; the subluminal class does not raise it
- SILENT: [Everett and Roman 1997: the superluminal subway](../theories/physics/everett-roman-superluminal-subway.md). Hawking's result that closed timelike curves require WEC violation is cited, but whether chronology protection forbids the two-tube machine is not adjudicated; the authors say it forces one "to confront all the associated problems".
- SILENT: [Krasnikov 1998: the tube laid on the way out](../theories/physics/krasnikov-tube.md). Krasnikov cites Hawking 1992 for the energy condition consequence of compactly generated Cauchy horizons but does not adjudicate whether chronology protection forbids the two-tube machine; the question is open.
- SILENT: [Lentz 2021: hyper-fast positive-energy solitons](../theories/physics/lentz-solitons-2021.md). Chronology protection is not raised in the paper or in the conference reply
- SILENT: [NASA Eagleworks warp field interferometer](../theories/physics/eagleworks-warp-field-interferometer.md). Never mentioned
- SILENT: [Natário 2002: warp drive with zero expansion](../theories/physics/natario-2002.md). Chronology protection is not discussed; whether Hawking's back reaction forbids the two-bubble loop for this class is unsettled and uncomputed.
- SILENT: [Van Den Broeck 1999: the pocket geometry](../theories/physics/van-den-broeck-1999.md). Not discussed; the two-pocket time machine's semiclassical fate is unsettled.
- SILENT: [Warp drives in modified gravity](../theories/physics/warp-drives-in-modified-gravity.md). Neither paper mentions chronology protection; whether semiclassical back reaction in a theory with torsion or fourth-order gravity would forbid the two-bubble loop is unexamined.
- SILENT: [White et al. 2021: the worldline-numerics Casimir pattern](../theories/physics/white-casimir-nano-bubble-2021.md). Chronology protection is never mentioned
