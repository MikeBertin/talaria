# HAZ-3: Tidal limits on a traversable throat

*The throat is large, and the exotic matter bill (STA-2) scales with it.*

**Kind:** constraint · **Family:** HAZ (Hazards) · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-12

## The result

Tidal constraints on a human-traversable wormhole. Morris and Thorne require the tidal acceleration across a two-metre body to stay at or below one Earth g, which bounds the radial and lateral tidal terms by about (10^8 m)^-2 at the throat. The throat radius bound this gives scales with the traveller's speed through the throat; at one percent of c it is about 500 km

## What it forbids or costs

The throat is large, and the exotic matter bill (STA-2) scales with it

## Escape hatches

Slow traversal; designs with the tidal terms tuned down at the price of more exotic matter
