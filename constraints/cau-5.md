# CAU-5: The no-communication theorem

*Every "entanglement radio" and quantum ansible.*

**Kind:** constraint · **Family:** CAU (Causality) · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-12

## The result

No-communication theorem: measurements on one half of an entangled pair cannot transmit information to the other half. The statistics of one party's results are unaffected by anything the other party does

## What it forbids or costs

Every "entanglement radio" and quantum ansible

## Escape hatches

None within quantum mechanics. Nonlinear modifications of quantum mechanics would allow signalling and are constrained for that reason (Gisin 1990, Polchinski 1991, not yet verified here)

## Who it bites

**Physics proposals**

- VIOLATES: [Entanglement signalling and the no-communication theorem](../theories/physics/entanglement-signalling.md). This is the entry that closes the proposal. The radio requires Bob's statistics to depend on Alice's operation; the partial-trace argument shows they cannot. Nonlinear quantum mechanics would reopen it (Gisin 1990, Polchinski 1991, both verified here) and is excluded for that reason (Simon, Bužek and Gisin 2001)
- VIOLATES: [Retrocausal signalling proposals](../theories/physics/retrocausal-signalling-proposals.md). The scheme requires Bob's singles statistics to depend on Alice's choice. The partial trace shows they do not; Cramer and Herbert's case-by-case analysis finds the fringes and anti-fringes always cancel
- SATISFIES: [ER = EPR (Maldacena and Susskind 2013)](../theories/physics/er-equals-epr.md). The paper invokes the no-communication theorem directly ("no local operation on one member of a entangled pair can influence the other") and builds the identification on it; the bridge is the geometric face of a correlation that carries no information.
- SATISFIES: [Gao, Jafferis and Wall: traversable wormholes via a double-trace deformation (2017)](../theories/physics/gao-jafferis-wall-2017.md). The teleportation reading needs the coupling as its classical channel; entanglement alone (the undeformed thermofield double) transmits nothing, in agreement with the no-communication theorem.
- SATISFIES: [Maldacena, Milekhin and Popov: traversable wormholes in four dimensions (2018), and the humanly traversable sequel (2021)](../theories/physics/maldacena-milekhin-popov-2018.md). The entangled-black-hole reading transmits nothing faster than light; the fermion exchange that generates the coupling is itself an ordinary field propagating through the outside.
