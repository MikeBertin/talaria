# Work or drive name

*One sentence saying what this drive is and where it comes from. It is pulled
verbatim into the generated index, so write it to be read there.*

**Kind:** fiction · **Loophole:** L4 · **Notability:** N1 · **Coherence:** F2 · **Causality:** C-silent · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-12

## The drive as depicted

What the work actually shows or says, from the primary material. Not what the
fan wiki has rationalised since. Two or three paragraphs. Where the work is a
franchise, name the instalment where the drive is defined.

## Origin and lineage

Who wrote it, when, and what they were reading. Whether it names or borrows a
real proposal. Whether it fed one. Link the lineage page where one exists.

## Which loophole, and why

The class from [GRADING.md](../../GRADING.md), with the reasoning. Where the
work uses two moves, say which is primary and why.

## The rules it keeps

Every stated limitation the work applies consistently. This section justifies
the **Coherence:** mark.

## What it ignores

What the register would ask and the work never answers. This section
justifies the **Causality:** mark. Absence is a finding: "never addressed in
any instalment" belongs here.
