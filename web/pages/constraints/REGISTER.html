TALARIA/atlas/constraints

The constraint register

Every published result that puts a price on beating light, with what it forbids and who it bites.

Status: v2.2 VERIFIED (M1, 2026-09-12; refined during M2; the M2 additions verified in a second pass the same day). Every citation was checked against the arXiv record, the journal DOI record or INSPIRE, and every stated result against the paper's abstract or text, by four independent verification passes. ✓ marks a verified entry; a residual ⚠ v1 inside a row names the one item in it that could not be opened (paywalled books, two 2026 preprints found by search). M1 corrections: Einstein 1907 was the wrong paper; two energy-condition attributions in ENE-2; the ENE-5 gloss of the 2021 critique; the STA-1 and HAZ-2 account of the interior flux; the Winful attribution and Scharnhorst magnitude in LOR-3 and LOR-4; OPERA was corrected, not retracted; LOR-5 split into the WRP family.

ENE-1The energy conditions. Null (NEC): energy density seen along any light ray is non-negative. Weak (WEC): energy density seen by any observer is non-negative. MAN-1Extra dimensions, where they appear in physics, are compact (Kaluza–Klein; large and compact in ADD, with Standard Model fields localised on a wall) or warped (Randall–Sundrum, a slice of anti-de Sitter space with the Standard Model on a brane). Reading the register

Three entries do almost all the work. CAU-1 says any FTL is a message to the past unless something breaks Lorentz invariance. ENE-2 says any geometric FTL costs negative energy. CON-2 says the negative energy has to be laid along the route in advance at sublight speed. A proposal that answers those three has answered the field; the rest of the register is what it costs once it has.

The register is deliberately physics-internal. It does not score plausibility, engineering readiness or taste. Those live in the dossiers.
