TALARIA grading scheme

Two catalogues, one spine. Every entry, real or invented, is first sorted by the loophole it uses. Physics entries then carry a standing and a bill. Fiction entries carry a notability, a coherence and a causality mark. Every empirical statement on any page carries a confidence and a source tier. The health checker rejects anything outside these vocabularies.

Loophole classes (shared)

ClassNameThe move
L1Faster in the same spaceThe object itself outruns light in ordinary spacetime
L2Move the spaceSpacetime is engineered to carry the ship; locally nothing exceeds c
L3Take a shortcutTwo distant points are connected by a tunnel, fold, gate or jump
L4Leave the manifoldThe ship enters another space with different rules and re-emerges
L5Send the messageInformation, not matter, is claimed to beat light
L6Not FTLFiled here by habit: time dilation, hibernation, reactionless thrust, sublight epics

A proposal that uses two moves (Mass Effect lowers mass to run L1 and uses relays for L3) gets a dossier per move, or one dossier with the primary class in the metadata and the secondary named in the text.

Physics: standing

What kind of thing the proposal is, as the literature stands today.

MarkMeaning
K4Exact solution of the Einstein field equations, or an established result of quantum field theory, peer reviewed
K3Peer-reviewed proposal with worked mathematics that is not an exact solution, or an exact solution whose central claim is contested in print
K2Speculative physics with a named mechanism: a new field, particle or dimension, without consensus mathematics
K1A claim with no worked mechanism, or a misreading of a real effect
K0Refuted, retracted or shown to be an artefact

K4 is a statement about the mathematics, not about feasibility. Alcubierre's metric is K4 and has a bill nobody can pay.

Physics: the bill

What the proposal needs that we do not have. A dossier may carry several.

MarkMeaning
B-negNegative energy density; violation of one or more energy conditions
B-massEnergy or mass beyond any conceivable engineering, even if positive
B-newA field, particle or dimension not in the Standard Model or general relativity
B-bootNeeds FTL to make FTL: the structure must be laid along the route in advance
B-causProduces closed timelike curves, or requires a preferred frame to avoid them
B-noneNo exotic requirement. Nothing faster than light sits here, and that is the finding

Fiction: notability

MarkMeaning
N1Canonical. Shaped how the public imagines FTL: Star Trek, Star Wars, Dune, Interstellar
N2Significant. Widely known and mechanically articulated: the Expanse, the Culture, Mass Effect, Vinge
N3Notable niche. Historically first, unusually rigorous, or an unusually clean example of a class

The cap is roughly sixty works. A drive earns a dossier by having an articulated mechanism, not by being famous.

Fiction: coherence

How the work handles the physics it invokes. This is the grade that matters.

MarkMeaning
F4Engages. Names a real constraint and builds story around its cost. Vinge, Haldeman, Le Guin, Interstellar
F3Acknowledges. States a limitation and applies it consistently: warp factors, navigators, relays only between relays
F2Rule-bound. Internally consistent rules with no physical content: hyperspace lanes, jump cooldowns
F1Handwave. Works when the plot needs it to
F0Joke. The drive is the punchline and knows it

F0 is not a low mark. The Infinite Improbability Drive is a perfect F0.

Fiction: causality

Any FTL signal in a Lorentz-invariant world is a message to somebody's past. What does the story do about it?

MarkMeaning
C-ownsAdmits the implication and uses it, or forbids FTL where it would bite
C-dodgesA stated rule prevents it: a preferred frame, jumps that are simultaneous for everyone, a hyperspace with its own clock
C-silentNever comes up

Constraint verdicts

Every dossier scores every register entry. Physics vocabulary:

VerdictMeaning
SATISFIESThe proposal respects the constraint as stated
VIOLATESThe proposal requires the constraint to fail, and says so or can be shown to
DODGESThe proposal evades the constraint by an assumption the constraint does not cover (a preferred frame, a pre-built route, a different theory of gravity)
SILENTThe proposal does not address it and the literature has not settled whether it applies
N/AThe constraint does not bear on this class of proposal

Fiction vocabulary, because a story cannot violate a theorem:

VerdictMeaning
ENGAGESThe work addresses this constraint on screen or on the page
RULEThe work has a stated rule that happens to answer this constraint
SILENTNever addressed
N/ADoes not bear on this class of drive

Confidence

[HIGH] peer-reviewed and unchallenged, or primary text read directly. [MED] peer-reviewed but contested, or a strong preprint, or a secondary source of good standing. [LOW] preprint only, press account, or a fan source with no primary confirmation.

Source tiers

TierMeaning
S1The paper itself, or the primary text, film or game where the drive is defined
S2Peer-reviewed review, monograph (Visser 1995, Lobo 2017), or an official technical manual
S3Franchise wiki, author or showrunner interview, reputable popular science
S4Press coverage
S5Forum, fan speculation, unsourced blog

Never blend the tiers. A Memory Alpha paragraph is S3 however confident it sounds, and a physics claim resting on S3 is [LOW] until an S1 is found.
